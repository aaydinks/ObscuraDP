# Code of conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
The full text at that link applies to everyone who takes part in this project's issues, pull requests,
discussions and any other project space.

In short:

- Be respectful and assume good intent.
- Criticize ideas and code, not people.
- No harassment, insults, or unwelcome personal attention of any kind.
- Respect privacy. Never post someone's personal information, and never share real personal data in an
  issue, a test, or an example. Use synthetic data.

## Reporting

If someone's behaviour here makes this project unwelcoming, report it through the contact form at
<https://obscuradp.org/#contact>. Reports are kept confidential. The maintainer will review every report,
reply to the person who made it, and may warn, temporarily exclude, or permanently exclude the person
responsible, following the enforcement guidelines of the Contributor Covenant.
